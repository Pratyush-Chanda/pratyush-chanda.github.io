DEVELOPER BIO

PROFILE
Technology enthusiast focused on Linux, operating systems, Android firmware, privacy-oriented computing and AI-assisted software prototyping. Interested in understanding how computing systems work, customising them and exploring alternatives to closed or restrictive platforms.

SHORT BIO
I explore Linux and alternative operating systems, customise desktop environments and boot configurations, and develop personal software concepts with AI-assisted implementation. My longest-used Linux daily driver has been Void Linux, which I used for approximately a year; I have also daily-driven Artix Linux and Nobara. My longer-term interest is in affordable, developer-friendly and repairable computing hardware, especially development boards and smartphones.
